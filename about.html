Our Journey

From a Travel Beginning
to Complete Travel Solutions.

ATC Holidays began its journey in 2007 as a tourism and travel service provider, built around a simple purpose: helping people travel with comfort, confidence and peace of mind.

As the years went by, we continued to grow and develop, expanding our services to meet changing travel and transportation needs. Today, our offerings extend beyond holiday planning to include cab and coach rentals, group and school transportation, corporate travel and hospitality services.

Although our services have evolved, our commitment remains the same — dependable support, thoughtful planning and better experiences for every journey.
