Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about exploring South India with ATC Holidays.

Planning Your Next South Indian Getaway?

Whether you're dreaming of Kerala's peaceful backwaters, Munnar's misty mountains, Ooty's scenic hills or Coorg's beautiful coffee plantations, find answers to common travel questions here.

General Travel Questions

What destinations does ATC Holidays cover?

ATC Holidays focuses on beautiful South Indian destinations, including Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. Popular destinations include Munnar, Alleppey, Wayanad, Ooty, Coorg and other scenic locations across South India.

Do you provide customised South India tour packages?

Yes! You can customise your trip based on your preferred destinations, travel dates, budget, number of travellers and travel preferences. Contact our team to discuss your requirements.

Which is the best time to visit South India?

The ideal travel season depends on your destination. October to March is generally popular for hill stations and sightseeing. Monsoon months can also offer beautiful greenery and refreshing landscapes, especially in Kerala and Karnataka.

Destinations & Packages

What are the popular Kerala tour destinations?

Some popular Kerala destinations include Munnar, Alleppey, Wayanad, Thekkady, Kumarakom and Kochi. Visitors can enjoy hill stations, backwaters, waterfalls, wildlife and beautiful beaches.

Do you organise Munnar and Ooty tour packages?

Yes. Munnar and Ooty are popular South Indian hill station destinations. Depending on availability and your requirements, you can enquire about packages covering tea gardens, viewpoints, scenic drives and local attractions.

Can I visit multiple South Indian destinations in one trip?

Yes. You can plan multi-destination journeys combining places such as Munnar and Alleppey, Ooty and Coonoor, or Coorg and Wayanad. Our team can help organise a suitable itinerary.

Do you arrange family trips and group tours?

Yes. You can enquire about family holidays, group tours, school trips, friends' getaways and other travel arrangements. Packages can be planned according to group size and travel requirements.

Booking & Payments

How can I book a tour package?

Browse our available tour packages and contact us through the enquiry form or WhatsApp. Share your destination, preferred dates and number of travellers. Our team will guide you through the booking process.

How early should I book my South India trip?

We recommend enquiring a few weeks in advance. During peak holiday seasons, festivals and school vacations, booking earlier is advisable to improve your accommodation and travel options.

Are accommodation and transportation included in the packages?

Package inclusions depend on the selected itinerary. Accommodation, transportation, sightseeing and other services may be included based on your chosen package. Please confirm the inclusions before booking.

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Cancellation and rescheduling conditions depend on the package and booking terms. Please contact our team before making changes to your reservation to understand the applicable conditions.

Travel Support & Assistance

Do you provide travel assistance during the trip?

Our travel support team can assist with travel-related enquiries and coordination according to the services included in your booking. Contact us for details about the support available during your journey.

What should I carry for a South India trip?

Carry comfortable clothing, walking shoes, personal medicines, identification documents and essential toiletries. For hill stations such as Munnar, Ooty and Coorg, carrying warm clothing is recommended.

How can I contact ATC Holidays?

You can contact us through the website's Contact Us page or WhatsApp button. Our team will help you with package enquiries, travel planning and booking-related questions.
